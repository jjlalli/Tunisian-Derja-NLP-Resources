# Tunisian Arabic NLP Resources

**An open, maintained inventory of every NLP resource that exists for Tunisian Arabic
(Derja / Tounsi / تونسي, ISO 639-3 `aeb`).**

👉 **Full map, and how to add to it: https://github.com/jjlalli/Tunisian-NLP-Resources**

Tunisian NLP has a findability problem more than a quantity problem. People build good
things — a dataset, a fine-tune, a benchmark — and they end up on a lab page or a personal
account, so the next person spends weeks rebuilding what already existed. This is the
place those things get recorded.

## What's in it

| | Entries |
|---|---|
| Text datasets, benchmarks, lexicons & papers | 66 |
| Speech corpora (ASR, SLU, translation, TTS) | 24 |
| Pretrained models (LLMs, encoders, ASR, TTS) | 14 |
| Researchers, labs & companies | 30 |
| **Total** | **134** |

Every entry carries an honest access tag: **90 open**, 11 paywalled, 10 paper-only,
9 on request, 2 gated. A resource that exists but can't be downloaded stays on the list
with the truth attached, rather than being quietly dropped or oversold.

Multi-dialect and pan-Arabic resources are included, with the Tunisian portion described
rather than the whole thing counted as Tunisian.

## Add your work

If you have built something for Tunisian Arabic, or you know something missing:

- **[Add a resource](https://github.com/jjlalli/Tunisian-NLP-Resources/issues/new?template=add-resource.yml)** — paste a link; formatting and verification are the maintainer's job
- **[Report something wrong](https://github.com/jjlalli/Tunisian-NLP-Resources/issues/new?template=correction.yml)** — a wrong entry is worse than a missing one
